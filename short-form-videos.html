Why Short-Form Video Matters

Short-form video offers the best organic reach of any content format. While traditional posts reach 2-5% of your followers, viral short-form content can reach millions of people who've never heard of your brand.

Brands that master short-form video build massive audiences, create cultural relevance, and drive traffic without paying for every impression. It's the closest thing to "free advertising" that exists today.
