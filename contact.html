FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with NovaVisuals.

What's the typical turnaround time?

Turnaround varies by project. Simple content can be delivered in 3-5 days, while larger productions typically take 2-4 weeks. We'll provide a specific timeline in your proposal.

Do you work with brands outside the US?

Yes! We work with e-commerce brands globally. For video production, we can arrange on-location shoots or work with local production partners in your area.

What if I'm not happy with the content?

We include revision rounds in all our packages and work closely with you throughout the process. We're not satisfied until you are—that's our guarantee.

Can you work with my existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely. We ensure all content aligns with your existing brand identity, color palette, tone of voice, and visual style guidelines.

Do you offer ongoing content packages?

Yes! Many clients work with us on monthly retainer packages for consistent content creation. We offer discounted rates for ongoing partnerships.

What do I need to provide?

For most projects, we'll need your products (for product shoots), brand guidelines if available, and clear communication about your goals. We handle the rest!
